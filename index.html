About

I am a master's student in Computational Science and Engineering (CSE) at the Georgia Institute of Technology, working in the LiDAR Lab under the supervision of Ye Zhao.

My research focuses on hardware systems for humanoid robots, spanning mechanical design, fabrication, hardware integration, and experimental evaluation.

Selected Projects

LATUMI: Large-area Tactile-based UMI

Formerly WT-UMI · 2026

A wearable whole-body tactile interface for learning contact-rich humanoid manipulation from human demonstrations and robot teleoperation. The system combines tactile sensing with force-supervised planning to handle bulky and deformable objects and support human–humanoid collaboration.

My contributions: I led hardware prototype design and contributed to sensor testing and integration, hardware testing, data collection, and analysis.
